Willpower is a limited, unreliable resource. Systems aren't. The most consistent savers tend to rely on automation, not motivation.

## The one-time setup that keeps paying off

- Automate a transfer to savings for the day after payday
- Route a fixed percentage of any bonus or windfall straight to savings, before it hits your regular checking account
- Use a separate bank for savings so it's slightly less convenient to spend

<Callout type="note">
  Studies on automatic enrollment in retirement plans show the same pattern
  again and again: default behaviors beat willpower-dependent ones almost
  every time.
</Callout>

## Start smaller than feels meaningful

If $200 a month feels impossible, start with $20. The goal in month one isn't the amount — it's proving to yourself that the system works. Increase it once it feels automatic rather than effortful.
