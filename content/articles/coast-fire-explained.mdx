Most financial independence conversations sound like an all-or-nothing sprint. Coast FIRE is the quieter middle path.

## The core idea

Once your retirement savings are on track to grow to your full retirement number on their own — without another dollar added — you've reached "Coast FIRE." From there, you only need to earn enough to cover current expenses; retirement is already taking care of itself.

<Callout type="note">
  Coast FIRE isn't about retiring early. It's about removing the pressure to
  save aggressively for retirement once compound growth has taken over.
</Callout>

## Why it appeals to a lot of people

- It reduces financial anxiety without requiring an extreme savings rate
- It opens the door to lower-stress, lower-paying work that's more fulfilling
- It rewards starting early — time in the market matters more here than almost anywhere else

## The tradeoff to be honest about

Coasting works because compound growth does the work over decades. Start it too late, and the math simply doesn't have enough runway left. It's a strategy that rewards starting in your 20s and 30s far more than your 50s.
