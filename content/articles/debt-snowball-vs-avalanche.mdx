Two popular methods dominate debt payoff advice, and they optimize for different things.

## The two methods, side by side

| Method | Order of payoff | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Snowball | Smallest balance first | Needing quick, motivating wins |
| Avalanche | Highest interest rate first | Minimizing total interest paid |

<Callout type="tip">
  The "best" method mathematically is almost always the avalanche. The best
  method for you is whichever one you'll actually finish.
</Callout>

## How to choose

If you've started and abandoned a debt payoff plan before, the snowball's early wins are often worth the extra interest cost. If you're motivated by numbers and unlikely to lose steam, the avalanche saves real money.

Either way, the biggest lever isn't the order — it's the total amount you put toward debt each month. Pick a method, automate the minimums on everything else, and put every spare dollar toward the target debt.
