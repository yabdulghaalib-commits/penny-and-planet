Not every "green" home upgrade is worth the cost. Here are seven ranked roughly by how quickly they pay for themselves.

## Fastest payback

1. Draft-proofing doors and windows — often pays back within a single season
2. LED lighting throughout — pays back in months, not years
3. A programmable or smart thermostat — pays back within a year for most households

<Callout type="tip">
  Start with the cheapest fixes first. Sealing drafts before upgrading
  insulation avoids paying to heat air that's just going to leak out.
</Callout>

## Longer payback, still worth it

4. Attic insulation — multi-year payback, but meaningful ongoing savings
5. Energy-efficient windows — long payback period, but improves comfort immediately
6. A heat pump water heater — payback varies widely by climate and local incentives
7. Solar panels — the longest payback, but often the largest total savings over 20+ years

<Callout type="warning" title="Check local incentives first">
  Many of these upgrades qualify for tax credits or utility rebates that can
  cut years off the payback period — always check before assuming the sticker
  price is the real cost.
</Callout>
