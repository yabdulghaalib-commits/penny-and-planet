Plenty of "eco-friendly" products are marketed at a premium without actually saving you money. These are the swaps that genuinely pay for themselves.

## Swaps with a real payback

- A reusable water bottle over bottled water — pays back within weeks for most people
- Cloth napkins and towels over paper — pays back within a few months
- A programmable thermostat — reduces both energy waste and your bill
- Air-drying laundry, even part-time — meaningfully cuts electricity use

<Callout type="tip">
  Start with whichever swap replaces something you buy most often. The
  payback is fastest wherever your current spending is most frequent.
</Callout>

## Skip the ones that don't pencil out

Not every trendy "sustainable" product is worth it. If an eco-branded version costs significantly more than the conventional option and won't be used enough to offset that cost, it's fine to skip it — the more sustainable choice is often just using what you already have for longer.
